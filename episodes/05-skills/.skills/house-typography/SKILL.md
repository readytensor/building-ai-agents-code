# House typography

Documents are typed with straight quotes, plain hyphens and three dots. The
rendered text follows these rules; they differ from common defaults, so apply
them as written.

## Quotes

- A straight double quote `"` becomes an opening quote `“` at the start of a word
  (after a space, the start of the text, or an opening bracket) and a closing
  quote `”` everywhere else.
- A straight single quote `'` follows the same rule with `‘` and `’`. Inside a
  word it is an apostrophe and becomes `’`: `don't` is `don’t`.

## Dashes

- A hyphen with a space on both sides becomes an en dash with a space on both
  sides: `Paris - Berlin` is `Paris – Berlin`. (Not an em dash.)
- Two hyphens become an en dash with no spaces, for ranges: `1990--1999` is
  `1990–1999`.
- Three hyphens become an em dash with no spaces: `wait---no` is `wait—no`.
- A single hyphen inside a word stays as typed: `well-known`.

## Ellipsis

- Three dots become the ellipsis character: `Well...` is `Well…`.

## Where the rules do not apply

- Inside code spans and code blocks, nothing changes.
- Inside link and image addresses, nothing changes.

## Examples

| Typed | Rendered |
|---|---|
| `She said "no" - twice.` | She said “no” – twice. |
| `It's 1990--1999, roughly---or so...` | It’s 1990–1999, roughly—or so… |
